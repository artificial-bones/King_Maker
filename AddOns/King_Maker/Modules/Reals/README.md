# Reals (new in King Maker 2.13)

Tarnished Undermine Reals come from the level 55+ dungeon bosses: each boss gives each character one a day. This page
shows which bosses still have today's Real for you, and reminds you to loot it.

It's the **Farming > Reals** page of the King Maker window (`/reals` or `/km reals`).

## The page

| What | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Tiles | Reals looted today, bosses still to loot (and in how many dungeons), Reals held and the cap, the time to the daily reset |
| Character | Whose Reals to show. Each character is read when you play it |
| Pop-up switch | The "loot your Real" pop-up on or off |
| Dungeons (left) | **Green**: at least one boss still has today's Real for you. **Red**: you've looted them all. Looted / bosses |
| Bosses (right) | The clicked dungeon's bosses: **green** Real waiting, **red** looted (and when), grey optional or no Real seen |

## In the dungeon

- **A boss goes down** and its Real for today is still yours to take: a pop-up and a chat line, *"A Real dropped on
  Withermaw: don't forget to loot it!"* (*"may have a Real for you"* until one has been seen on that boss). Once a
  visit per boss. It also comes when you target or mouse over a dead boss you can still loot.
- **You close the loot with the Real still in it**: *"You left a Real on Withermaw"*.
- **You leave the dungeon** with bosses killed but their Real not taken: one line naming them.
- Click the pop-up to open the page; right-click to dismiss it.

## How it knows

- The loot window says which corpse each item is from, so a Real is credited to that exact boss when you take it
  (or when the window closes and your Reals went up).
- The day ends at the daily reset, 10 a.m. Eastern (US daylight saving included); then every boss is green again.
- **The boss list corrects itself.** It starts with every non-rare boss of level 55 and up in Sunken Temple, Blackrock
  Depths, Lower and Upper Blackrock Spire, Dire Maul (all three wings), Scholomance, Stratholme (both sides),
  Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace. A boss a Real comes from that isn't on it is added (*learned*); a boss looted 3
  times with no Real and none ever seen stops counting (grey) until one drops. Summoned and event bosses are
  optional: counted once you loot a Real from one.
- It only knows what you looted with King Maker running.

## Settings (Settings > Reals)

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Pop-up when a boss has a Real to loot | on | The pop-up and chat line in the dungeon, and the "left a Real" warning |
| Remind me when I leave with Reals unlooted | on | One line when you leave, naming the bosses |

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/reals` | Open the page (also `/real`, `/km reals`) |
| `/reals status` | Looted today, what's left per dungeon, held, the reset, in chat |
| `/reals notify on\|off` | The pop-up |
| `/reals leave on\|off` | The leaving reminder |
| `/reals forget First Last` | Forget a character |
| `/reals debug` | What the game reports (for bug reports) |

## For other addons: `RealsKing` (API_VERSION 1)

| Function | Returns |
|---|---|
| `RealsKing.GotToday(npcID[, charKey])` | true when that character looted the boss's Real today |
| `RealsKing.Today([charKey])` | Reals looted today, bosses left, dungeons with any left |
| `RealsKing.Held([charKey])` | Reals held and the cap, from the last reading |
| `RealsKing.SecondsToReset()` | seconds to the daily reset |
| `RealsKing.RegisterCallback(owner, fn)` | `fn("CHANGED")` after anything changes; `UnregisterCallback(owner)` |

The boss list is generated by `tools/build_reals.py` from the DungeonJournal addon's data (thanks to Exehn).
