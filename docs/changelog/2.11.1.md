# King Maker 2.11.1

The Scan button says what it's going to do.

## Changed
- **The Scan button's label says what a click does**: **Full scan** when the game allows one, or **Quick (full in
  9m)** while the game's 15-minute wait between full scans runs. The label counts down by itself, and when the wait
  is over with the auction house open, it turns back into **Full scan** and chat tells you.
- **A quick scan says why.** Clicking Scan during the wait prints why it's a quick scan and when the next full scan
  is possible. The wait is shared by all your characters, so if the last full scan ran on an alt, chat names it.
  Same on the Prices page's Scan button.
