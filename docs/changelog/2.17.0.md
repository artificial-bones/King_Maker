# King Maker 2.17.0

## New: what gear disenchants into, in the tooltip
- Every green, blue and purple piece of gear now shows its **Disenchant** value and, under it, what it gives on
  average: each dust, essence and shard with its chance, the average amount and what that's worth at today's prices
  after the auction house cut. The rows add up to the Disenchant value; the rare shard or crystal is marked (rare).
- It shows on the short tooltip too, without holding Shift.
- A result nobody has a price for yet still shows (no price), and the total says it's missing some.
