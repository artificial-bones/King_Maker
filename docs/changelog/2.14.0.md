# King Maker 2.14.0

**Shopping is gone.** It tried to do too much and didn't work well; buying tools will be built as their own addons.
The quick part stays, and is simpler: your **favorites beside the auction house**.

## Fixed: the realm hotfix
Forever's November hotfix (realm IDs hidden to stop layer-hopping addons) makes the game report an empty realm name.
King Maker files every character under "First Last-Realm", so since the hotfix it was quietly starting fresh entries
under "First Last" (inventory, cooldowns, rested, reals, reputation, Crafting Profits), a second Profit & Loss pool
with no realm, and a blank guild list for the Blacklist.
- King Maker now remembers your realm (and reads it back from your saved characters when the game won't say it).
- At login, everything saved under the realm-less names is folded back into your real characters. In Profit & Loss
  the stray pool's "opening stock" is dropped (it counted stock you already had) and its mail to your alts is
  marked as a move between alts.
- The Blacklist's guild list comes back the same way.

## New
- **Favorites at the auction house.** While the auction house is open, a small panel beside it lists your favorites
  A to Z, with each one's lowest price and 7-day trend.
  - **The button on top** adds the item the auction house is showing (the one you searched or clicked) to your
    favorites. On an item that's already a favorite, it removes it.
  - **x** on a row removes that favorite.
  - **Click an item** to search it in the auction house, ready to buy there as usual.
  - **Graph** opens its Price History page: the graph, the 7- and 30-day averages, the low and the high. Hover a row
    for its tooltip with the same numbers.
  - They're the same favorites as the stars in Price History. Close the panel with x; the **Favorites** button beside
    the auction house brings it back. Turn it off in Settings > Price History or with `/ek panel`.

## Fixed: the disenchant value in tooltips
- The **Disenchant** row and the **DISENCHANT** verdict now take the auction house's 5% cut off what the dust and
  essences sell for, the same as the auction house price, so the verdict no longer leans toward disenchanting.
- Gear nobody has listed now shows King Maker's section too, with its disenchant value and whether vendoring or
  disenchanting pays more.

## Fixed: the daily reset
- Supply Writs and Reals now count down to the real daily reset, **10 a.m. Eastern** (it follows US daylight saving),
  instead of the time the game reported. Done orders and looted bosses turn green again at 10 a.m.

## Removed
- The **Shopping** page and its auction house panel, `/shop`, and the `ShoppingKing` API for other addons.
- What fed it: Cooldowns' **Shopping: a week of mats** button and its "Cooldown mats" list (with its two settings),
  the Profession panel's **Add missing to Shopping**, Profit & Loss's **Add to the shopping list**, and the
  tooltip's **Shopping list** row (`/sk tip goal`).
- Profit & Loss's Holdings filter now shows **All items** or **Favorites** instead of a Shopping list.

Your old shopping lists aren't deleted: they stay in your saved data, King Maker just doesn't show them.
