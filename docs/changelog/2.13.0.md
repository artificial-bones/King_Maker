# King Maker 2.13.0

A new **Reals** page under Farming (`/reals`) for Tarnished Undermine Reals: each level 55+ dungeon boss gives each
character one a day, and now you can see which ones you still have to get.

## New
- **Dungeons in green or red.** Green while at least one boss still has today's Real for you, red once you've looted
  them all, with looted / bosses for each. Click a dungeon for its bosses: green waiting, red looted (and when).
- **Exact, boss by boss.** The loot window says which corpse a Real is on, so it's credited to that boss when you
  take it.
- **Don't forget to loot it.**
  - A pop-up and a chat line when a boss with a Real for you goes down (once a visit).
  - A warning if you close its loot with the Real still in it.
  - When you leave the dungeon, one line naming the bosses whose Real you didn't take.
  - Turn the pop-up off with the switch on the page, in Settings > Reals, or `/reals notify off`. The leaving
    reminder has its own switch. No sound, just the pop-up.
- Tiles for Reals looted today, bosses still to loot, Reals held against the cap, and the time to the daily reset.
  Pick any character to see theirs. `/reals status` says it all in chat.
- **The boss list corrects itself.** It starts with the non-rare level 55+ bosses of Sunken Temple, Blackrock Depths,
  both Blackrock Spires, the three Dire Maul wings, Scholomance, both sides of Stratholme, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's
  Terrace. A boss a Real comes from that wasn't listed is added; one that's looted three times without a Real, and
  never showed one, stops counting until one drops.

It only knows the Reals you looted with King Maker running, so the first day shows everything green.
