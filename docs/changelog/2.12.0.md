# King Maker 2.12.0

Prices for what you loot, from your guild.

## New
- **What drops is priced by your guild.** When you loot something in a dungeon or while farming that you have no
  price for, or only one over 6 hours old, King Maker asks your guild for it. Most drops (greens, blues, recipes,
  odd materials) are only priced by full scans, so now whoever ran the last one fills them in for you. The price shows
  in the tooltip and the Farm Tracker within seconds, as long as someone online has it.
  - Quiet by design: half a minute of drops goes out as one small message, each item is asked about once a session,
    and grey items, quest items and items that bind when picked up are skipped.
  - On by default. Settings > Scanner > "Ask the guild for prices of what you loot", or `/sk sync loot off`.

## Changed
- **Anyone holding a price can answer a request**, not only the person who scanned it. Prices passed on keep the
  scanner's name, and nobody gets their own price sent back. Price History's "Ask guild" gets answers more often too.
