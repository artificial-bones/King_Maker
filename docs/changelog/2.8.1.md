# King Maker 2.8.1

## Removed
- **Guild bests are gone.** The Farming Ledger's Guild tab, its three settings and `/farm guild` are removed: your
  farms are your own. Nothing about your farming is sent to your guild any more, and the guildmates' bests your
  client had received are deleted the first time you log in. Your own bests (the **BEST** mark and the "new best
  gold per hour here" line) stay as they were.
