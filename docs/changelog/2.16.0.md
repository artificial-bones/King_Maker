# King Maker 2.16.0

**Reputation, Supply Writs and Reals are gone.** They didn't fit what King Maker is for, making and tracking gold,
so they're out to keep it focused.

## Removed
- The **Reputation** section: the Reputation overview, the **Supply Writs** page, their Home tiles and settings,
  `/rep`, and the `ReputationKing` API for other addons.
- The **Reals** page (Tarnished Undermine Reals from dungeon bosses): its loot pop-ups and reminders, Home tile and
  settings, `/reals`, and the `RealsKing` API.

Your old reputation and Reals data isn't deleted: it stays in your saved data, King Maker just doesn't show it.

## Fixed: crafting cooldowns after a loading screen
- Changing zones could set off a "Refined Salt is ready" reminder (and briefly show it ready) while the salt still had
  hours left: for a moment after a loading screen the game reports the Salt Shaker as off cooldown. A "ready" that
  disagrees with a timer the game already gave is now only believed once it has held for 10 minutes, so no more false
  reminders. A cooldown running out still reminds on time.
