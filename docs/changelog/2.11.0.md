# King Maker 2.11.0

Guild price sharing now keeps everyone on the newest scan, not just the people who were online when it ran.

## Changed
- **Logging in catches you up.** King Maker remembers the newest scan you hold, yours or a guildmate's. When you
  log in it asks the guild who has a newer one. The guildmate online with the freshest scan answers (only one
  does, so no flood) and sends you every price newer than yours in the background, including prices it got from
  someone else. A morning scan now reaches people who log in at night, as long as anyone online has it.
  - Each price keeps the name of whoever scanned it, even when a third guildmate passes it on.
  - If your scan is the newest, guildmates say so and it goes to the guild once.
  - A chat line says what arrived: *Synced 412 newer price(s) from Bob (Ann's scan, 25 min ago).*
  - The Prices page's **Guild sharing** tile shows the newest scan you hold and whose it is; `/sk status` too.
- **Requests go first.** Price History's "Ask guild" and other quick messages are sent ahead of a catch-up that's
  still trickling out.

## New
- **Share my full scans** (Settings > Scanner, or `/sk sync full on`): also pass on every item of a full scan, not
  only Trade Goods. Off by default: a whole auction house is about 290 messages, a few minutes in the background.
- `/sk sync pull` (and a Settings button) asks for a newer scan by hand. It happens by itself at login.

## Fixed
- After a `/reload`, guildmates answered your login only once per session; now again after a minute.
- A price stamped far in the future (a guildmate's wrong clock) is ignored instead of winning forever.

Guildmates on 2.10 or older still get your prices after each scan and still send you theirs, but only 2.11 passes
on other people's scans, so the catch-up gets better as the guild updates.
