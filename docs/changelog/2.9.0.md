# King Maker 2.9.0

King Maker is about gold: the market and managing your money. This release turns the profession window's panel
into a profit tool and retires the skill-up planner.

## New
- **The profession window panel is a profit quick view.**
  - **This recipe**: the profit per craft and its margin up front, with one verdict (CRAFT, DISENCHANT, SELL RAW,
    SKIP). Below it: what the mats cost, what it sells for after the 5% cut, the disenchant alternative, the 30-day
    average and 7-day trend, how many are listed, how fast it sells and how many to make. With Profit & Loss on, it
    also shows your profit **at what you actually paid** for the mats in your bags, and your last week's sales of it.
  - **All crafts** (was Best now): every recipe you know in that profession, ranked by **Profit now** (profit per
    craft times how many the auction house will take), with the margin, how many your bags can make, how many are
    listed and how many to make. Sort by any column, show only what you **can make now**, or search by name.
  - A **Buy mats / Use my mats** switch in the panel's header.

## Changed
- **Crafting Profits' two buttons have plain names**: **Buy mats** (the materials are a cost: flips) and **Use my
  mats** (you already have them: is crafting worth more than selling them raw?). `/mk mode buy` and `/mk mode mine`
  (`farm` still works).

## Removed
- **Skill-ups** (the page, `/suk`, its settings and the crown menu entry), the skill-up line on the profession
  panel and in recipe tooltips, and the panel's "Next skill-ups". The game's own profession window already colours
  every recipe.
  - Your profession skills, known recipes and vendor prices moved into Crafting Profits by themselves: the crown's
    tooltip still shows your skills, and Cooldowns and the item tooltip's "who can make it" still use them.
  - The old **Skillup_King** folder is no longer part of the download.
  - About a third of a megabyte less data to load at login.
