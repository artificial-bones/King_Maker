# King Maker 2.10.0

Two new parts of King Maker: a guild-wide blacklist, and the first page of a Reputation section.

## Guild Blacklist

A new **Guild** group in the sidebar with one page, **Blacklist** (`/bl`, `/blacklist`, `/km blacklist`): players
your guild doesn't want to group with, on one list shared by every guildmate running King Maker.

- **Anyone can add, edit or remove.** Right-click a name in chat, a portrait, a party or raid frame, an enemy player
  or a Who list line: **Add to Blacklist** (or **Blacklist: edit note** / **Remove from Blacklist** for someone
  already on it). **Alt-click** a group finder listing to blacklist its leader. Or press **Add** on the page, or
  `/bl add First Last: note`. Every entry has a reason (Scam, Ninja loot, Toxic, Other) and a required note of up to
  160 characters. Guildmates and yourself can't be added.
- **Warnings before anything happens.** When a listed player joins your group, or invites you, a red warning in the
  middle of the screen, a sound and a chat line with the note (once per player per session; after a `/reload`, one
  "already in your group" line instead). A red count on the sidebar while one is in your group.
- **Marked everywhere.** A big red **BLACKLISTED** before their name in chat (on by default; a setting turns it off),
  a red line with the reason and note in their tooltip, a red name and **BLACKLISTED** in the group finder (and how
  many listed members a group has, named in its tooltip), and in the Who list.
- **The page**: tiles (on the list, added this week, last added, last in your group, sharing, guildmates heard
  from), a search, the list newest first with sortable columns, and a detail view with the whole note, who added it
  and when, the last edit, the last time they were in your group, **Edit** and **Remove** (asks first).
- **Shared lightly.** Changes go out at once; at login one fingerprint is compared and only the entries that differ
  travel (at most 6 messages a minute). The latest change wins; a removal is remembered for 60 days so nobody who
  missed it can bring the player back. Only the list is ever sent: names, reasons, notes, who and when. Each guild
  has its own list; without a guild it's your personal list and nothing is sent. `/bl share on|off`.
- **Settings > Blacklist**: share with my guild, warnings (join, invite, sound), chat tag, tooltip line, group
  finder marks, forget removed entries after 60 days.
- Nothing is ever kicked or declined (the game doesn't let addons do that): it warns and marks only.
- For addon authors: `BlacklistKing.IsListed(name)`, `Get(name)`, `GetAll()`, `RegisterCallback(owner, fn)`. Saved
  in `BlacklistKingDB`.

## Reputation: Supply Writs

A new **Reputation** section in the sidebar, for the factions that pay in recipes, Favor and gold. The first is your
side's supply faction: the **Azeroth Commerce Authority** (Alliance) or **Durotar Supply and Logistics** (Horde).
`/rep` (or `/reputation`, `/km writs`).

### New
- **Reputation** (overview): every tracked faction with its standing, a bar, rep to the next standing, rep a day at
  your 7-day pace, days to go and what the next standing unlocks, plus a rep chart. More factions can plug in later.
- **Supply Writs**:
  - **Today**: orders done today, orders you hold and can still turn in (a **red count** by the page), rep today and
    the reset countdown.
  - **Your orders**: each order you hold with the item to deliver, what it costs to buy or craft (whichever is
    cheaper), the rep, rep per gold after the coin pouch, and the customer and zone. Click for a waypoint (TomTom, or
    the game's map pin).
  - **Cheapest rep**: sealed writs on the auction house ranked by gold per rep point, with the receipt path (Forged
    Delivery Receipt + Waylaid Crate) beside them.
  - **Progress**: standing, pace, days to go, Merchant's Favor and what the next standing unlocks.
  - **Favor shop**: the faction's 311 recipes and their Favor cost, with Crafting Profits' profit per craft and per
    Favor point.
  - **All orders**: all 150, filtered by tier, profession, held or not done today.
  - **Characters**: every character's standing, writs done today, rep today, orders held and Favor.
- A **login reminder** when you hold orders you haven't turned in today, a **Needs your attention** line on Home,
  writs done today on Home's character cards, and **Supply Writs** in the crown's right-click menu.
- **Settings > Reputation**: the sidebar count, the login reminder, your Diplomat perk rank.
- Customers the data doesn't know yet (51 Expert and Artisan orders) are written down the first time you turn one in.
- `ReputationKing` API for other addons (`GetStanding`, `DoneToday`, `IsDoneToday`, `HeldCount`, `GetOrder`,
  `GetFavor`, `SecondsToReset`, `RegisterFaction`, `RegisterCallback`).

### Changed
- **Quest Gold no longer lists Craftsman's Writ quests** (six were in its data as "new in Forever"): they're
  repeatable profession dailies, tracked on the Supply Writs page now. Quest Gold: 847 quests in 38 zones.
