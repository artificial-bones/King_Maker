# King Maker 2.18.0

## Crafting Profits is easier to use
- **Profession tabs** replace the tick boxes. They all start lit (All). Click one to see just that profession; after
  that each click turns a profession on or off. **All** lights them all again. The second row of Best / profession
  tabs is gone: the list shows whatever is lit, with the profession in the Skill column when more than one is.
- **Search**: type any part of an item's name ("cloak", "iron") to filter the list. Escape clears it.
- **Every recipe shows**, the ones you don't have greyed out. The All recipes / My recipes / Patterns I lack button
  is replaced by one **Hide unlearned** tick. A saved "Patterns I lack" goes back to showing everything.

## Quest Gold removed (for now)
- The Gold > Quest Gold page, `/qk` and its crown action and Home card are gone: it didn't work well enough yet. It may
  come back later. Your learned quest gold stays saved (QuestKingDB is kept), so nothing is lost if it returns.

## Mat Prices removed
- The Farming Ledger's **Mat Prices** tab and the Farm Tracker's **Prices** tab are gone: Price History and the
  Prices page already show what mats sell for.

## White Items removed
- The Farming > White Items page, `/fk white` and its Home card are gone. Mob Farming is unchanged.

## Home: the numbers instead of Best right now and Characters
- **Top items, 30 days** replaces Best right now: the items that made you the most (realized profit with Profit &
  Loss, auction profit from the Sales Ledger without it). Click one to go there.
- **The numbers, 30 days** replaces the Characters strip: net worth change, realized profit (or auction profit),
  return on cost with the average time to sell, sell-through, farming gold per hour, then average sale and what your
  stock would make if sold now, as many as fit. Hover a number for how it's worked out; click it to go there.

## Profit & Loss is easier to read
- Overview has four tiles: **Profit** (and its return on cost), **Sales** (and the average sale), **Stock worth** and
  **If sold now** (with what's underwater). Sold with cost unknown and used or gone, which never count in profit,
  moved to one muted line under them; hover it for what they are.
- The item list is about what you sold: how many, average price, average cost, profit, ROI (free for farmed units),
  how long you held one before it sold, and how many you still hold. Items you only hold are on Holdings.
- Trades: dates, item names and where units came from no longer get cut off.

## The neutral auction house
For players who trade at the goblin auction houses in **Gadgetzan, Booty Bay and Everlook**. None of it shows until you
scan one, so nothing changes for everyone else.
- **Scanning there keeps its own prices.** The button says **Neutral full scan** / **Neutral quick scan**. Before, a scan
  at a neutral auction house wrote over your faction's prices. Crafting Profits, Inventory, Profit & Loss, Price History
  and guild sharing keep using your faction's prices only.
- **Prices page: Faction AH / Neutral AH.** Neutral shows its prices next to your faction's with the difference, and two
  tiles: **Buy on neutral** (cheaper there than it sells for at home after the cut) and **Sell on neutral** (pays more
  there even after its 15% cut). Click one to list those items. The page opens on the house you're standing at.
- **Tooltips**: a **Neutral AH** line with its price, how it compares with yours and how old it is.
- **Sales Ledger**: sales, purchases and expired auctions from a neutral auction house are recognised (by the 15% cut,
  the sender, or where you posted). An **All houses / Faction AH / Neutral AH** button splits the ledger by house, and
  neutral lines carry a small grey **N**.
- **Inventory**: On AH keeps your auctions on both houses. Opening one no longer wipes the other's from the count. Hover
  an item for how many are neutral.
- `/sk neutral on|off|clear`, and `/sk house auto|neutral|faction` if King Maker ever guesses the house wrong (it tells
  by the zone).

## New look
- King Maker's icon is now gold coins instead of the crown: on the minimap button, the window's logo,
  Home and the addon list. The Prices page has a new icon of its own so the two don't look alike.

## Fixed
- Inventory > Items: the **Worth** column was cut off ("458..."). The other columns are a little narrower and Worth is
  compact, so it fits.
- Lists no longer look like they keep scrolling when you're already at the top (Trades) or the bottom (Holdings):
  rows that tie on the sort column (same sale time, no price) kept swapping places on every mouse-wheel turn. Fixes
  every King Maker list.
