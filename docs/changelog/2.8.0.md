# King Maker 2.8.0

Everything since 2.5.3. Versions 2.6 to 2.8 were built together and ship as this one release; each part
below is one piece of it, newest first.

## Profit & Loss follows your stock

### New
- **Profit & Loss follows your stock through crafting, disenchanting, mail, quests and trades.**
  - **Crafting carries the cost.** What you make costs what its mats cost you (oldest or newest first, as you chose),
    so a bandage sale shows a real profit instead of "cost unknown", and its linen no longer sits in "used or gone".
    Mats with no lot count at their worth then; thread, vials and other vendor reagents at what a vendor charges.
    "Create all" counts as one batch. An enchant, or something you make that binds to you, has nothing to sell: its
    mats' cost goes to Used or gone.
  - **Disenchanting splits the item's cost** over the dust, essences and shards by what each is worth. A green you
    looted was free, so its mats are too.
  - **Mail to your alts is "in transit"**, never a gap: nothing closes while it waits in their mailbox. Mail to anyone
    else is gone at once ("mailed to Name").
  - **Quest rewards** become lots that cost nothing, and **trades** count: what you get costs the gold you gave, what
    you give is a sale for the gold you got.
  - Anything it can't see (another addon's craft queue or mail sender) works as before: the mats go "used or gone"
    for 30 days and the product is untraced.
- **Listed at**: hover something you hold on Holdings for how many are on the auction house and what you asked
  ("listed at 3g 10s: +38% if they sell").
- **Search by name** on Profit & Loss's Trades and Holdings tabs.
- **Inventory**: **Avg cost** and **If sold** columns on the Items tab, from Profit & Loss (who holds it moves to the
  item's tooltip).

### Changed
- Profit & Loss's **Where it came from** lists **Crafted** and **Vendor, trades**; Trades > By buy marks lots
  **Crafted**, **DE**, **Quest** and **Trade**.
- Rolls you win in a dungeon, and master loot handed to you, become farmed lots like any other loot.

## Profit & Loss, guild bests, Home overview

### New
- **Profit & Loss.** A new page, first in the Gold group: what every trade actually made. Every auction house
  purchase (from any window or addon), everything you loot and what you buy from vendors becomes a lot; every sale
  uses lots up and its profit is what it brought after the 5% cut minus what those units cost you. `/pnl` or
  `/profitloss`.
  - **Overview**: realized profit, what you'd make selling what you hold now, stock at cost and what's underwater;
    beside them, never in them, stock that was used or gone and sales no purchase covered. Realized per day, where it
    came from (auction buys, farming, opening stock, vendors) and every item you sold or hold.
  - **Trades**: each sale and the lots it used; each purchase and what used it (closed, part or open); what left
    without a sale.
  - **Holdings**: what you hold at cost and at today's price; underwater items are marked, with the price that breaks
    even. Filter by Shopping list (Oracle's lists too).
  - **Day one** reads your whole Sales Ledger, so the flips you've already made are matched straight away. What you
    own beyond that counts as opening stock at today's value.
  - **Oldest stock first (FIFO)** by default, **newest first (LIFO)** one click away. **Farmed items count as free**,
    or at their value when looted. Switching works every number out again.
  - Your characters on one realm and faction share their stock: mail between them isn't a sale. Stock that leaves
    without a sale is "used or gone", undone if it turns up again within 30 days.
- **Your cost in item tooltips**: "Your cost: 2g 10s each (40) +45% if sold" (Settings > Profit & Loss).
- **Guild bests.** A new **Guild** tab on the Farming Ledger: the best gold per hour at each farm (a zone and what
  you did there) across your guild, who set it (in their class colour), when, its top item, and your own best beside
  it. Filter by Mobs, Gathering or Mixed and **Near my level**; click a farm for everyone's best there.
  - Only each character's personal best per farm is shared (its top 20, sessions of 10+ active minutes), never your
    sessions, and only with guildmates running King Maker.
  - Light on the guild channel: one short message when you set a new best, one fingerprint at login, and only the
    farms that differ are exchanged. At most 6 messages a minute.
  - Settings > Farm Tracker: **Share my best farms with my guild** and **Show guild bests** (both on), **Forget guild
    bests after** 60 days. `/farm guild on|off`.

### Changed
- **Sales Ledger**: a **P&L** column on the Sales tab, a **Realized P&L** line on the Summary (click it for the new
  page), and **Still holding** / **Net if sold now** now come from your lots instead of an estimate.
- **Home is now an overview of your gold.** Four tiles across the top: **Net worth** (with 30 days of bars and the
  change since a week ago), **P&L, 7 days**, **gold per hour farming** and **Cooldowns**. Click a tile to open its page.
  Below them: a **30-day chart** (net worth, or profit per day), **Needs your attention** (cooldowns ready, shopping
  deals, holdings underwater, items that don't sell, old prices, quest gold left in your zone, alts full of rest;
  click a row to go there), **Where your gold came from** in the last 30 days (sales by strategy, farming, cooldown
  crafts), **Best right now** (your most profitable craft, your best farm, the white item most worth hunting, the
  best quest zone), and a strip with each **character**'s gold, rest and cooldowns ready. Every part says how to fill
  it on a new install. The quick-action buttons are gone from Home; they're still in the crown's right-click menu.
  The crown's tooltip shows P&L too.
- **Mailbox**: the line when you close it says what the sales made: "3 sold for 4g 10s (P&L +1g 50s)".
- **Shopping**: what you hold of an item and what it cost you, in its tooltip, detail facts and the auction house
  panel.

### Fixed
- **Farm Tracker: greens you win in a dungeon now count.** With group loot, anything green or better is rolled on and
  goes straight to the winner's bags, so it never "left" your loot window and the tracker missed it. A roll you win,
  and master loot handed to you, now counts for the mob it dropped from (or "Unknown mob" if you never opened that
  corpse) once it reaches your bags, exactly once. What other players win never counts. Mob Farming's drop rates
  already counted what the window showed, whoever won, and still do.
- **Quest items that sell count.** A quest-type item with a vendor or auction price (Righteous Orb, for one) is
  valued and listed like any other drop; only quest items worth nothing are tucked into the tracker's "quest items"
  line.

## Farming trends, Shopping keep

### New
- **Farming Ledger: Trends.** Farmed per day, your average gold per hour, time farmed, your best zone, the top item
  and this week against last week. **Best farms** lists each zone and what you did there with its gold per hour over
  all its time; click one to see only those sessions. **Top items farmed** shows what made the most.
- **Farming Ledger: Mat Prices.** Mining, herbs, skins, cloth and enchanting mats: what one sells for now, how many
  are listed and the change against the 30-day average, next to what it's worth to *you*: when you last farmed it,
  how many an hour you get, and your best gold per hour in a session that farmed it.
- **Export your sessions** as text for a spreadsheet: the **Export** button on the Farming Ledger, or `/farm export`.
- **New best gold per hour here.** When a session beats your best at a farm, one line in chat says so, and the
  record holder gets a **BEST** pill in the ledger.
- **Shopping: Keep N in stock.** Switch an item from "Buy 20" to "Keep 20 in stock" (**Buy | Keep** in its editor,
  or `/shop keep Linen Cloth 20`). It then wants 20 minus what you own on this realm and faction, doesn't count down
  as you buy, and wants more again as you use them. The table shows "keep 20", the auction house panel buys what's
  missing, and the deal count follows. Bought items count toward the stock right away, even before you open the mail.

### Changed
- **Containers count once.** A clam, junkbox or lockbox you looted in a session and then opened counts as what was
  inside, not as itself and its contents too. Its session notes how many you opened.
- **Count disenchanting** now takes effect straight away on the session you're farming, and each saved session keeps
  the way it was counted when you change the setting later.
- Shopping list codes carry Keep amounts. Older codes still import as before.

## Smaller Price History

### Changed
- **Price History takes less than half the space**, so login and `/reload` are quicker. Each item's history is now
  saved as one short packed line instead of a line per scan. Your history converts itself the first time you log in,
  a few items at a time (chat says how much it saved); if you log out halfway, it carries on next time. The 7-day and
  30-day numbers stay exactly the same.
- **Older history is summed up as it ages**: every scan for 7 days, then a point a day to 30 days, a point a week to
  180 days, and a point per 4 weeks after that (graphs further back than 30 days show a point a week). Before, every
  item kept a point a day for 120 days, which grew the saved file by megabytes a month. Favorites still keep a point
  a day forever.
- **Items nobody has listed for 90 days are forgotten.** Favorites never are.
- Market and Material Scans no longer add a point when they only repeat what the scan a moment before saw.

### New
- **Settings > Price History** (it was "Econ"): how long to keep every scan, how long to keep a point a day, and when
  to forget items no one lists any more, plus **Compact now** and how much space the history takes.
- `/ek compact` sums up and forgets straight away and says what it saved; `/ek status` now shows the points kept
  and the space they take.

## Farm Tracker, Cooldowns, Shopping, zoom

### New
- **Farm Tracker and Farming Ledger.** One floating tracker for mob farming and all gathering: mob loot and coin,
  mining, herbs, skinning, fishing, pickpocketing, chests, clams and lockboxes. Start, Pause, Stop & save and Split;
  it pauses itself when you're idle and can start itself when you begin farming. Every session is saved: the new
  **Farming Ledger** page shows what each one made, gold per hour, a gold-over-time graph and where the money came
  from. `/farm`, or a key binding (Key Bindings > AddOns > King Maker). Drag its corner to resize it; the list grows
  with what you loot. It replaces the old farm tracker and farming ledger boxes (your old tallies are kept as one
  "Carried over" session).
- **Crafting Cooldowns.** Transmutes (one shared timer, Legionite included), Mooncloth and the Salt Shaker on every
  character: what's ready, what to make, profit per craft and per week, mats you own, a live "Cooldown mats" shopping
  list, and a reminder when one comes ready. A red number by the page (and on the minimap crown) when one is ready on
  the character you're on. `/cdk`.
- **Shopping, redone.** Deals first (green, biggest saving first), prices that fit, cost to buy what you still want,
  the 7-day trend, a detail pane with a price chart and a "use 7-day average" max. At the auction house the panel
  looks items up, shows how many are under your max, checks your gold, asks twice before spending a quarter of it,
  and steps to the next deal after each purchase (every buy is still your click). A chat line after a scan when
  items drop under your max (`/shop notify`). The "Import Oracle buy list" button is gone: Oracle's list shows by itself.
- **Zoom the window**: drag the dots in its bottom-right corner (70% up to what fits your screen). `/km size 120`,
  `/km size reset`.
- **Quest Gold looks up quest gold by itself** the first time each character enters a zone, and ships with every
  amount learned so far. The "Ask the game" button is gone.
- **Version check**: when a guildmate has a newer King Maker you get one line in chat. `/km version`.
- **Tooltips** across every page: what each number means and how it's worked out.

### Changed
- **Sales Ledger**: "Profit" is now **Net cash** (sales minus purchases minus deposits), and two new tiles show
  **Net if sold now** and **Still holding** (what you bought and still have, at today's price), so stocking up no
  longer reads as a loss.
- Gathering values use the same rule as everything else: the auction house after its 5% cut, or a vendor when that
  pays more.

### Fixed
- The Sales Ledger recorded some mails two or three times, inflating its totals. Old duplicates are removed once
  (chat says how many).
- Mob Farming and Gathering missed loot when fast auto-loot (Leatrix Plus, FasterLoot) was on.
- The window-size slider made the window drift, and 140% could push it off the screen.
- Quest Gold's zone list ran off the bottom of the window, and its "Best reward" column overlapped the gold columns.
