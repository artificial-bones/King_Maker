# King Maker 2.15.0

## New: Zones in the Farming Ledger
- A **Zones** tab (Farming Ledger) adds up every saved session in each zone, dungeon and raid: sessions, active time,
  farmed, gold per hour over all of it, your best session there, mobs per hour and when you were last there. Six
  tiles pick out your best zone, best dungeon, most farmed, most time and last farmed; **All / Zones / Dungeons**
  narrows the list.
- Click a place for its own page: what you did there, gold per hour against your farming everywhere, gold per hour
  of each session there, its sessions (Back from a session returns to the place) and its top items.

## Fixed: dungeons in farming sessions
- Sessions record the dungeon or raid you're in (`zi`, `zk`), because the game gives some dungeons no map
  (Stratholme showed as "Unknown zone") or the whole continent's.
- Sessions saved before this are placed by the mobs they looted, so old dungeon runs move to their dungeon. The game
  gives no map inside most dungeons, so a run used to count only the minutes outside and showed as the zone around
  the entrance (Scholomance as Western Plaguelands, Blackrock Spire as Burning Steppes). A session that spent most
  of its time with no map now goes to the dungeon or raid its mobs live in.
- Best farms, session titles, the "new best gold per hour here" line and the export use the same place name.
